About Honeyz

Beauty Crafted With Passion

Honeyz Salon n Makeup Studio is a welcoming beauty parlour, hair salon and bridal makeup studio located in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. Designed for women and brides who value understated elegance and attentive care, our studio brings together thoughtful beauty rituals, modern hairstyling and refined makeup artistry under one roof.

Every visit begins with listening—understanding your skin tone, hair texture, outfit palette and the mood of your celebration. From intricate bridal transformations and fresh rose-adorned hairdos to restorative facials, spa pedicures and everyday grooming, every service is delivered with patience, cleanliness and warmth.

Bespoke Bridal Artistry

Customised bridal, engagement and occasion makeup designed to photograph gracefully and feel comfortable all day.

Complete Hair & Beauty

From keratin treatments and copper highlights to hydra facials, manicures and gentle grooming with attentive staff.
